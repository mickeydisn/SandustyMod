[h1]📊 WORD STATISTIC — Live Dig-Site Census[/h1]

[i]What you dig. What you leave. What still sits under the dust.[/i]

Stop guessing how much gold is left in the mountain.
Open [b]World Statistic[/b], hit refresh, and get a full count of every element, terrain and structure on [b]authorized[/b] cells only.


[h1]🧰 THE TOOL[/h1]
[list]
[*] Equip the [b]World Statistic[/b] tool — the overlay appears while it is selected
[*] [b]Lock[/b] the panel so it stays open when you switch tools
[*] [b]Mini mode[/b] — compact card strip with live totals and trend Δ (last vs previous scan)
[*] Drag the panel; position, zoom and opacity are remembered per world
[/list]


[h1]🏠 HOME CARDS[/h1]
[list]
[*] Custom [b]resource cards[/b] (Gold, Sand/Dirt, Water by default)
[*] Pick any element, terrain or structure from the live catalogue
[*] First item on a card is primary (drives the card color)
[*] Per-item history graphs (same engine as the list tabs)
[/list]


[h1]📈 HISTORY & DIG %[/h1]
[list]
[*] Up to [b]20[/b] stored refreshes + a permanent [b]reference[/b] baseline (first scan)
[*] Elements / Structures / Terrains tabs: multi-series graph over the last 20 samples
[*] Tick list rows to choose which series appear on the chart
[*] [b]Terrains only:[/b] [i]% dug vs reference[/i] — how much of the baseline dirt is already gone
[/list]


[h1]⚙️ SETTINGS[/h1]
[list]
[*] [b]Mod enabled[/b] — master switch. When off: tool, overlay and mod storage are cleaned up
[*] [b]Auto refresh[/b] — scan after load, then on a timer (1–20 minutes, default 10)
[*] Panel ⚙️ tab: lock, zoom, opacity, auto refresh interval (saved in mod storage)
[/list]


[h1]✨ DETAILS[/h1]
[list]
[*] Counts respect [b]authorization[/b] — cells you cannot interact with are skipped
[*] Built-in / mod filters on the list tabs
[*] Zero-count rows stay hidden
[*] Toast on every sync start and finish
[/list]
