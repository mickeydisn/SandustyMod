[h1]🎲 RANDOM ARTEFACT — Roll for Loot[/h1]

[i]Feed a hungry machine. Get a mysterious artefact. Watch it spit treasure and disappear.[/i]

Somewhere in your world is a generator that eats metal and sand, does absolutely
not tell you what it is making, and hands you a glowing mystery object that
pumps out a random element until it runs dry and blinks out of existence.

That is the whole game. Bring one. Feed it. Catch what it drops.


[h1]🚀 FIRST STEPS[/h1]
[list]
[*] Open the build menu and pick the [b]Artefact[/b] category
[*] Place an [b]Artefact Generator[/b] — only one per world, so choose your spot well
[*] Give it room. Hover it with your mouse and a purple square shows exactly where artefacts can appear
[*] Feed it. Its tooltip tells you what it wants: [b]Need 42 more (8/50) · eating Copper ×0.5[/b]
[*] When the tooltip says [b]READY[/b], [b]click the generator[/b]
[/list]

That is the whole loop. It gets good fast.


[h1]🔨 WHAT YOU CAN BUILD[/h1]
[list]
[*] [b]Artefact Generator[/b] — the machine. Max 1. Eats one material, summons artefacts
[*] [b]Terrain Collector[/b] — the big brush. Max 1. Paints giant terrain patterns
[*] [b]Artefact Gold Link[/b] · [b]Copper Link[/b] · [b]Sand Link[/b] — three little lamps that tell you what the generator is currently eating
[/list]

The [b]Random Artefact[/b] itself is never in your menu. It is not yours to place,
pick up, move or demolish. If you try, the game ignores you. That is the point.


[h1]🍽️ FEEDING THE GENERATOR[/h1]
The generator picks [b]one[/b] material at random each cycle and eats only that one.
Drop the others in and it will politely ignore them.

[list]
[*] [b]Copper[/b] — the fast lane. Worth double per cell
[*] [b]Gold[/b] — the middle of the road
[*] [b]Sand[/b] — the expensive one. Heavy, slow, takes forever
[/list]

[b]It eats from inside its own footprint only.[/b] 

[i]Rule of thumb: to force what it eats, only give it that one material. To keep it
happy, keep all three stocked and let it choose.[/i]


[h1]🔌 THE MATERIAL LINKS[/h1]
The three link pads are tiny, and they are the reason this mod is more than a toy.

Each one lights up and sends an [b]ON[/b] signal while the generator is eating its material. That means the generator is effectively broadcasting its appetite — and you can read it.

[list]
[*] Want it to always eat copper? Watch the [b]Copper Link[/b] and top up only when it goes dark
[*] Automate the feeding with signals and you never have to touch it again
[*] Power the generator with a signal and it fires artefacts [i]by itself[/i] the moment it charges — no clicking required
[/list]

Place one of each link and you have a working supply-chain sensor for free.


[h1]💎 CATCHING AN ARTEFACT[/h1]
[list]
[*] It appears [b]below[/b] the generator, never on top of it. Keep the ground below clear
[*] A purple flash marks the spot and the camera peeks at it for a moment
[*] The spawn needs elbow room. Structures or restricted tiles nearby and it simply will not appear
[*] It clears a small circle around itself when it lands, so keep loose items out of the way
[*] [b]Max 5 alive at once.[/b] Let them run dry before you summon more, or the next one is skipped
[/list]

Once it is up you just leave it alone. It spins, it glows, and it works.


[h1]🌀 THE ARTEFACT[/h1]
[list]
[*] It decides what it is at random from [b]Sand, Red Sand, Wet Sand[/b] and [b]Seed[/b]
[*] It holds between [b]100 and 500[/b] — a random amount, revealed only after it lands
[*] It spits elements out in a spinning spray, roughly [b]15 to 75 seconds[/b] of work
[*] The spiral tells you how it is doing: [b]green[/b] means plenty left, [b]red[/b] means about to vanish
[*] When it is empty it removes itself. Nobody has to clean up
[/list]


[h1]🖌️ THE TERRAIN COLLECTOR[/h1]
Charges on its own schedule, independently of the generator, with its own random
material. When its tooltip says [b]READY — click to stamp terrain[/b], click it.

A [b]64×64[/b] pattern is painted on your world from a randomly fetched icon —
the shape in [b]dirt[/b], a [b]moss[/b] outline, and a cleared ring around the outside.

[list]
[*] Every stamp is different. Every single one
[*] It lands somewhere within about 100 cells of the collector, so leave the neighbourhood open
[*] No free 64×64 patch? Nothing is spent and your charge is kept. Try again somewhere flatter
[/list]

Cover a continent in giant icons if you are feeling committed.


[h1]💡 GOOD TO KNOW[/h1]
[list]
[*] [b]One machine each.[/b] A second generator or collector is simply refused
[*] [b]Charge is saved.[/b] Close the game mid-charge, come back, keep going
[*] Reading tooltips pays off — remaining, current material, live artefact count and status are all on the generator's hover card
[*] Artefacts cannot be deleted early, so if you want the loot, stand there and watch it work
[*] Turning the mod off removes its buildings and clears its saved data. Its [b]Enabled[/b] switch is the only setting
[/list]
