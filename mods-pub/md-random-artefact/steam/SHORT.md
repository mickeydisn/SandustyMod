[h1]🎲 RANDOM ARTEFACT — 30-second summary[/h1]

[i]FEED → CASINO → LOOT (fully automatable)[/i]

[h1]THE MINDMAP[/h1]
[list]
[*][b]🍽️ GENERATOR[/b] (max 1) — picks ONE random material per cycle → eats it from its own floor → charges to 50 → [b]CLICK or SIGNAL[/b] → spawns an artefact
[*][b]🔌 MATERIAL LINKS[/b] (gold / copper / sand) — light up + send an [b]ON[/b] signal while the generator is eating that material
[*][b]💎 ARTEFACT[/b] (never placeable) — random element, random haul (300–1500), spins & sprays, green→red aura, empties itself and vanishes ([b]max 5 alive[/b])
[/list]
[i]▼ the machine broadcasts its appetite — read it, feed it, never touch it ▼[/i]

[h1]WHY YOU HAVE TO TRY THIS[/h1]
[list]
[*][b]Slot-machine loop, zero busywork[/b] — you build the [i]pipeline[/i], not the payout: keep all 3 materials stocked, the links tell you what's being eaten [i]right now[/i], and a signal auto-fires artefacts the instant they're charged → [b]fully hands-off[/b]
[*][b]The Links are free sensors[/b] — top up only when a link goes dark, or wire the generator to a signal line and it summons artefacts [i]by itself[/i]
[*][b]Randomness is the reward[/b] — what it eats, what the artefact is, how much it holds: all rolled. You automate the [i]odds[/i], not the outcome
[*][b]One of each[/b] — a single generator/collector makes placement a real decision
[/list]

[h1]🖌️ BONUS: TERRAIN COLLECTOR (max 1)[/h1]
Side toy, independent charge → click when READY → stamps a [b]64×64[/b] Dicebear icon on the world: [b]dirt[/b] fill, [b]moss[/b] outline, cleared ring. Every stamp different. Optional fun — the loop above [i]is[/i] the mod.

[h1]CHEAT SHEET[/h1]
[list]
[*][b]Generator diet[/b] — Copper ×0.5 (fast) · Gold ×1 · Sand ×5 (slow). It eats only its chosen one
[*][b]Fire it[/b] — click, [b]or[/b] a signal = no clicking ever
[*][b]Artefact spawn[/b] — below the generator, needs clear ground, max 5 live
[*][b]Artefact life[/b] — spiral green = full → red = dying → self-deletes
[*][b]Cleanup[/b] — empty artefact removes itself; disabling the mod wipes its data
[/list]
