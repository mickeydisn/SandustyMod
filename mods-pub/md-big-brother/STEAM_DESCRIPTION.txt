[h1]👁️ BIG BROTHER — Live CCTV Cameras & Screens[/h1]

[i]He sees you. Now you see what HE sees.[/i]

Tired of not knowing what's clogging on the other side of your factory?
Slap down a [b]Camera[/b], hang a [b]Screen[/b] on your wall, and watch it [b]LIVE[/b].


[h1]📷 ➜ 🖥️ HOW IT WORKS[/h1]
[list]
[*] Place a [b]Camera[/b] — it captures a live square zone around itself
[*] Place as many [b]Screens[/b] as you want — every Screen paints that channel's live feed, every frame
[*] [b]CLICK the Camera[/b] to swing its capture corner: [i]top-left → top-right → bottom-right → bottom-left[/i]
[*] No camera on a channel? Screens show [b]NO SIGNAL[/b] instead of a frozen frame
[/list]

[code]Pro tip: Build a dark room full of Screens = instant security office.
[/code]

[h1]⚙️ SETTINGS[/h1]
[list]
[*] [b]Channels (1-10, default 4)[/b] — how many Camera/Screen pairs exist. Takes effect without restart.
[*] [b]Zone size in tiles (2-12, default 6)[/b] — how big an area each camera sees. Bigger = better overview, smaller = juicy detail.
[*] [b]Mod enabled toggle[/b] — turn it off without unsubscribing. Feeds drop to NO SIGNAL.
[/list]

[h1]✨ FEATURES[/h1]
[list]
[*] [b]Truly live[/b] — feeds re-capture ~10x per second from the real world map
[*] [b]Schematic view[/b] — per-cell colors, crisp pixels, no smoothing blur
[*] [b]Placement preview[/b] — see the zone outline + screen feed while you're still holding the ghost
[/list]
